# Skill: negotiation prep brief

WHEN to use: before any pricing call or when the supplier revises terms and a
one-pager is needed for the user or the VP.

Steps that worked:
1. Pull current terms + latest quote from memory.md and the quotes-comparison
   CSV in artifacts/ (update the CSV first if the quote is new).
2. Compute the delta vs. our target AND vs. budget ceiling - always both.
3. Lead with the recommendation in one sentence ("counter at X because Y").
4. Three leverage points max, each with its evidence source (dashboard export,
   benchmark contract, spend history). More than three dilutes the ask.
5. End with the walk-away position and the next calendar checkpoint.
6. Save as artifacts/YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>-brief.md; navy/gold HTML version only
   when it goes outside the category team.

Pitfalls:
- Do not quote raw email text in the brief; distill (data-hygiene rule).
- Escalation percentages compound - show year-3 absolute $, not just the %.
- SLA leverage only works with the actual uptime export attached; TEL disputes
  verbal numbers.
