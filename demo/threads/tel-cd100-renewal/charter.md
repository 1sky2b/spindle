Negotiate the 3-year renewal of the TEL service contract covering the 12 CD-100 coater/developers in Fab 1. Current contract expires Oct 31. Land at or below a 3% annual escalation while fixing the response-time SLA.

## Expectations
- One-page negotiation briefs: recommendation up top, numbers below, navy/gold identity
- Keep a running quotes-comparison table in artifacts/ and update it whenever TEL revises terms
- Weekly status line I can paste into the Thursday category review

## Be proactive when
- TEL sends revised terms or pricing (analyze the delta against our target the same day)
- A benchmark from another supplier's service contract becomes relevant

## Escalate to me when
- Any term exceeds 2% above the budgeted envelope
- TEL proposes reducing scope (tools, coverage hours, parts inclusion) to hit a price
- Anything would slip the timeline past Oct 1 (legal needs 4 weeks)
