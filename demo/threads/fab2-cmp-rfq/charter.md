# Fab 2 CMP tool RFQ

## Purpose
Run the RFQ for two CMP tools for the Fab 2 ramp (install target Q2 next year). Score bids on TCO, not sticker price, and get to a defensible recommendation for the capital review board.

## Expectations
- Scorecard-driven: every vendor claim gets a score and a source, no vibes
- TCO horizon is 7 years: purchase + install + consumables + service + footprint

## Be proactive when
- A bidder revises their offer or asks a clarifying question others should also answer
- Consumables pricing assumptions look inconsistent with what we pay in Fab 1

## Escalate to me when
- Any bidder proposes an exclusivity or bundling clause
- The recommendation would differ from the lowest-TCO option for any reason
