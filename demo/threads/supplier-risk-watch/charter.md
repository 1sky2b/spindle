# Supplier risk watch

## Purpose
Standing watch on supply risk across the key suppliers (TEL, Lam, Screen, Brooks): financial health, lead-time drift, trade/export-control changes, and anything that threatens the Fab 2 ramp.

## Expectations
- Distilled inbox items only - one screen, source-tagged, with a "so what" line
- Monthly one-paragraph risk summary I can forward to the VP unedited

## Be proactive when
- A watched supplier appears in trade-restriction or export-control news
- Lead-time signals drift more than 2 weeks on anything feeding the Fab 2 ramp
- A supplier's quarterly results flag margin pressure in their service/spares business

## Escalate to me when
- Any single-source part for the Fab 2 ramp shows a credible supply threat
- A risk item needs money or a second source qualified to mitigate
